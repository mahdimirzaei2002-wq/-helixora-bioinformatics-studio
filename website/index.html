THE RESEARCH TOOLKIT

A connected workflow. Seven focused modules.

Move between molecular data retrieval, sequence analysis and assay planning without leaving your research workspace.

⌕

Gene & Transcript Retrieval

Search NCBI transcripts by gene symbol and organism, retrieve RefSeq accessions and GenBank records, inspect annotations and load FASTA sequences.

NCBI · RefSeq · GenBank
⌁

Sequence Analysis

Inspect nucleotide sequences, review sequence length, export FASTA files and share active transcript records across modules.

FASTA · Shared registry
⌗

Variant Analysis

Evaluate nucleotide substitutions against transcript or CDS coordinates, predict coding consequences and generate transcript-level HGVS descriptions.

SNV · CDS · HGVS
◎

Public Variant Databases

Retrieve preliminary matching records from ClinVar and dbSNP for analyzed variants, with source records available for manual review.

ClinVar · dbSNP
◈

Gene & Protein Characterization

Explore gene and protein annotations, SNP information, protein domains, post-translational modifications, expression, literature and available structures.

UniProt · InterPro · GTEx · PDB
⟷

Primer Design

Retrieve or upload transcript sequences, design RT-qPCR primer candidates, assess primer properties and perform in-silico specificity screening.

RT-qPCR · Primer3
⌘

Transcript & Isoform Analysis

Catalogue transcripts, map transcript-to-protein records, compare sequences, inspect alternative splicing and identify transcript-discriminating regions.

Isoforms · Splicing · CDS
⇧

Connected Research Workflow

Use a shared active transcript record across compatible modules, keep analysis context together and export data for downstream work.

Integrated workspace

Scientific responsibility

Helixora supports exploratory bioinformatics workflows. Predicted variant consequences are not clinical classifications; ClinVar and dbSNP matches need manual review; primer candidates require specificity assessment and experimental validation. Transcript-discriminating regions are evaluated against the retrieved transcript set, not as genome-wide specificity proof.

For academic research and education only. Not intended for clinical diagnosis or treatment decisions.
